There are questions you can only answer if you were paying attention along the way. Not because the answer is hidden — because it doesn't exist yet. It only forms from watching things unfold over time.

Most memory systems store and retrieve. That works when the answer already exists somewhere. But the questions that matter most — about patterns, relationships, shifts in behavior — can't be retrieved. They have to be built from everything together, over time.

Adapt is built for those questions. It lives with your data and grows by it. Its structure on day one is different from its structure after a week, because it reshapes itself around what it encounters. You don't configure Adapt — it adapts.

Pretty much every project in our lab needed exactly this. A system that helps people understand themselves through their own data — noticing what they keep circling back to, what they avoid, how their priorities shift. A clinical co-pilot that tracks how a client's emotional framing evolves across months of sessions. A team assistant that notices knowledge gaps nobody anticipated and grows to cover them.

Adapt is fully open source under the MIT license.
