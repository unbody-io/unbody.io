Join us for an evening of building, learning, and connecting with fellow AI enthusiasts.

## Event Details

**When:** March 20, 2024, 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM
**Where:** Unbody HQ, Amsterdam
**Who:** Developers, designers, and curious minds interested in AI

We're hosting a hands-on hack night focused on practical LLM applications. Whether you're building your first chatbot or fine-tuning models for production, there's something here for you.

## What to Expect

The evening kicks off with a short intro to Unbody's API and some inspiration from projects built by the community. Then it's heads-down building time. We'll have mentors floating around to help debug, brainstorm, and push your ideas forward.

Pizza and drinks provided. Bring your laptop and an idea you want to explore.

## Projects from Last Time

At our last hack night, teams built everything from a recipe generator that works with whatever's in your fridge to a tool that summarizes Slack channels. The energy was incredible.
